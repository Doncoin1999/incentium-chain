The auction

Waiting for the chain…

Rules

PriceDutch auction. SN1 starts at 50,000 ICT, every later slot at 4 × what the previous one sold for. The price falls 25 % a day, smoothly per block, down to a floor. The first buyer at the current price wins.
Floor1st slot 1,000 ICT · 2nd–4th 500 · 5th–8th 300 · 9th and later 200
PaymentNot refunded. Half goes to the on-chain treasury, half is burned.
PaceOne slot at a time, 60 days between a sale and the next auction.
Cap8 subnets until 2029. Then the cap doubles by itself (16, 32 … 256) when every slot has been taken for 90 days, at least 75 % of subnets pay at least 10 miners, and 180 days have passed since the last raise.
LeavingNo subnet is voted out for being weak. A subnet that paid no 3 miners in any epoch for 30 days (after its 180-day immunity) can be removed by anyone. Incentium governance keeps an emergency brake against abuse.
Issuance10 % to the treasury. SN0 keeps at least half of all issuance; the other subnets share the rest by their stake, phased in over 180 days after SN1 opens. Each subnet pays 50 % to miners, 38 % to validators and 12 % to its owner.
ControlOne key, then optionally a multisig, then optionally its stakers as a DAO. Control only moves forward. Full rules in the technical specification, Section 4.
