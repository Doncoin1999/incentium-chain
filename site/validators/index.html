Validators

Anyone can produce Incentium's blocks or back someone who does. Every day the chain elects the block producers with the most ICT behind them. This page reads it all straight from the chain.

Produce blocks

  1. A Linux server: 4 cores, 8 GB memory, 200 GB disk, port 30333 open.
  2. Run the installer. It builds the node from the open source code, runs it and creates session keys with a proof that they are yours: 
    curl -sO https://git.incentium.org/incentium/incentium-chain/raw/branch/main/scripts/install-validator.sh
bash install-validator.sh mainnet YOUR_STASH_ADDRESS
  3. In your own wallet, signed by the stash: staking.bond (at least 100 ICT), session.setKeys with the keys and proof the installer printed, then staking.validate.

You earn a share of every day's reward pot by the blocks you write, after your commission is shared with those who back you. Cheating in block finality costs at least 10 % of the bond; a day without blocks takes you off the candidate list, without a cut.

Back a block producer

  1. Bond at least 10 ICT from your wallet: staking.bond.
  2. Nominate up to 16 candidates from the list above: staking.nominate.
  3. Rewards follow each day; unbonding takes 7 days.

Signing from this page comes later. Today use polkadot.js apps with Talisman or SubWallet. If a producer you back cheats, your bond is cut by the same share as theirs.

The rules

  • Election. Once a day the chain elects up to 20 producers by backing, with Polkadot's sequential Phragmén method, which spreads the backing evenly.
  • Reward. 2 % of all ICT issued (taken from the development fund's 10 %) and every transaction fee go to a pot that is paid out each day by blocks written.
  • Safety. Blocks become final when more than two thirds of the producers agree. If no candidate is elected, the producers of the day before carry on, so the chain never stops for lack of candidates.
  • The genesis keys. The four keys that started the chain keep reserved places until at least 7 independent producers have been elected every day for 30 days in a row. Then they leave by themselves, and no one can bring the reserve back.
