Mine Network Launch rules Code

Machine intelligence, checked and paid for.

Incentium is an open network where anyone can run an AI model, answer requests, and earn ICT for answers that pass verification. Validators re-run a sample of every miner's work on a reference model; only work that matches is paid.

Connecting to the testnet

Mine on the testnet

One command on a Linux computer with 4 GB of RAM installs the model, creates your miner key and registers it. You need no ICT, no open port and no GPU.

curl -sSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doncoin1999/incentium-chain/main/scripts/install-miner.sh | bash

Your key is saved in ~/.incentium-miner/miner-key.json. Back it up: whoever has it controls the miner's rewards. Test ICT has no value.

How a reward is earned

  1. Tasks go out

    Each epoch, validators publish prompts. Your miner fetches them and answers with the network's reference model.

  2. Answers are signed

    Every answer is signed with your miner key, so nobody can answer in your name.

  3. A sample is re-run

    Validators re-run a share of the answers and compare the model's probabilities. A miner running a different model is reported and stops being paid.

  4. The epoch settles

    Validators commit their scores, reveal them one epoch later, and the chain pays out: 60% to miners, 30% to validators, 10% to the fund.

Launch rules, in the open

There is no investor or team allocation, no company and no token sale. Every ICT is issued by the protocol, apart from 100 ICT at genesis for the launch operator's transaction fees, which counts toward the cap.

The first 90 days

The launch operator registers the first miners and validators, without fees. On the testnet, anyone who runs the installer is registered for free through the operator.

Launch rewards are locked

Everything earned in the first 90 days goes to an on-chain vault: locked for two months after day 90, then released at 10% every two months.

At the end of day 90

The chain itself removes the operator's sudo key and opens two disclosed bounties from the fund: 25,000 ICT for marketing and up to 50,000 ICT for an exchange listing.

After that

Every change and every payment from the fund needs an on-chain vote by ICT holders. ICT still locked in the launch vault cannot vote.

Three phases

  1. Phase 1

    One subnet, one standard task: serving a fixed open model. Rewards follow verified work. The testnet runs this today.

  2. Phase 2

    Anyone can open a subnet with its own task and its own Alpha token, bought back with at least half of the subnet's issuance. Opens when the whitepaper's criteria are met: at least six months of operation, 250 miners, 16 independent validators, an external audit and an on-chain vote.

  3. Phase 3

    Issuance follows measured use: locked value, user revenue and fees, with caps that make manipulation cost more than it pays.
