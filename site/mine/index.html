Mine ICT on your computer

Your computer answers small AI tasks with an open Qwen model. Validators check the answers, and the network pays in ICT. Setting it up is one line, and nothing needs an administrator password.

MacWindowsLinux

On a Mac

  1. Open Terminal

    Press ⌘ Space, type Terminal and press Return. A window with text opens.

  2. Copy this line

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doncoin1999/incentium-chain/main/scripts/install-node.sh | bash

  3. Paste it and press Return

    Click in the Terminal window, press ⌘ V and then Return. It takes a few minutes. When it is done, your browser opens the Incentium dashboard, and an Incentium icon appears on your desktop.

  4. Follow the dashboard

    It finds the largest Qwen model your Mac can run and downloads it. Send the short request to join, and write down the 12 backup words. Mining starts by itself once you are approved.

On a Windows PC

  1. Open PowerShell

    Press the Windows key, type PowerShell and press Enter. A blue or black window with text opens. You do not need to run it as administrator.

  2. Copy this line

    irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doncoin1999/incentium-chain/main/scripts/install-node.ps1 | iex

  3. Paste it and press Enter

    Right-click in the PowerShell window to paste, then press Enter. It takes a few minutes. When it is done, your browser opens the Incentium dashboard, and an Incentium icon appears on your desktop.

  4. Follow the dashboard

    The same as on a Mac. With an NVIDIA graphics card the PC can run larger models; without one it runs the smallest model on the processor.

On Linux

  1. Open a terminal

    Ubuntu or Debian with at least 4 GB of memory. No GPU, open port or ICT is needed.

  2. Run this line

    curl -sSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doncoin1999/incentium-chain/main/scripts/install-miner.sh | bash

  3. Back up your key

    The key is saved in ~/.incentium-miner/miner-key.json. Whoever has it controls the miner's rewards, so keep a copy somewhere safe.

What you need

  • A Mac, a 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC or a Linux computer with at least 8 GB of memory (4 GB works, slowly). Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) or an NVIDIA graphics card runs larger models.
  • 3 GB of free disk, more for larger models.
  • The computer switched on, plugged in and online. Rewards follow the time it is available.

What it installs

Everything goes into one folder, ~/.incentium-node (on Windows %USERPROFILE%\.incentium-node): Python, llama.cpp and the Incentium node. It starts when you log in. The code is open on GitHub; read the install script for Mac or Windows before you run it.

Rewards in the launch period

For work in the first 90 days, a quarter of each reward is paid at once. Three quarters are locked and released in steps from day 150 to day 690.

Remove it

On a Mac, paste this in Terminal. Your key is copied to your home folder first.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doncoin1999/incentium-chain/main/scripts/install-node.sh | bash -s -- --uninstall

On Windows, paste this in PowerShell:

$env:INCENTIUM_UNINSTALL=1; irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Doncoin1999/incentium-chain/main/scripts/install-node.ps1 | iex

The node runs on the Incentium testnet while the network is being tested. While it mines, it can also answer visitors on Incentium Chat; you can switch that off on its dashboard.
