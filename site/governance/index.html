Governance

The tracks

TrackWhat it can doDecision depositTimingNeeded to pass
RootAnything, including runtime upgrades and protocol settings100 ICT1 day preparing, up to 7 days deciding, 1 day confirming, takes effect 7 days laterApproval from 100 % falling to 75 %; support from 50 % falling to 5 % of all ICT
TreasurerSpend from the treasury, any amount10 ICT1 day preparing, up to 7 days deciding, 1 day confirming, takes effect 1 day laterApproval 100 % → 50 %; support 25 % → 2 %
Small spenderSpend up to 1,000 ICT1 ICT4 hours preparing, up to 3 days deciding, 6 hours confirming, takes effect 1 day laterApproval 100 % → 50 %; support 10 % → 1 %
EmergencyOnly authorize a runtime upgrade that fixes a bug1,000 ICT1 hour preparing, up to 2 days deciding, 3 hours confirming, takes effect 1 hour laterApproval 100 % → 90 %; support 50 % → 20 %

Approval is the share of aye votes; support is the aye votes as a share of all ICT. Both thresholds fall in a straight line over the decision period, and a referendum passes once both are met and stay met for the whole confirmation period. Votes can be locked for longer to weigh more (conviction): 0.1× with no lock, then 1× to 6× for 1 to 32 lock periods.

Take part

Vote

  1. Open the referendum in polkadot.js apps with Talisman or SubWallet.
  2. Vote aye or nay with any amount of ICT, and choose how long to lock it.
  3. Your ICT stays yours; it is only locked for the period you chose.

Propose

  1. Anyone with ICT can submit a referendum on the track that fits (a small submission deposit, returned).
  2. Someone must place the decision deposit before it can be decided.
  3. A runtime upgrade is decided by its hash; after it passes, anyone can upload the code. The command-line tool governance.py does all of this.

Data from wss://rpc.incentium.org, read from the chain's storage. Any Incentium node gives the same answers. Times are estimated at 12 seconds per block. Add ?net=testnet to the address to see the testnet.
